## Model comparison

The Google Sheet is the source of truth for components in the 3D models. The comparison column below is a local audit of the default assemblies in `index.html` at commit `10806ec`, checked on 2026-10-03. The sheet’s values were unchanged when re-fetched for this audit.

**DIFFERENT** means another component or unlisted configuration is modeled. **MISSING** means the specified component is absent. **PARTIAL** means only part of the specification is represented. **FIT DIFFERENCE** marks a conceptual route that does not fit its stated cable length. **MATCH** covers component identity/purpose, not supplier CAD accuracy or tested ratings. **SHEET CONFLICT** distinguishes contradictory sheet details from model substitutions.

See [component differences and extra parts](component-model-audit.md) for source-code evidence and items modeled without a BOM entry. Candidate listings remain separate from the authoritative BOM.
